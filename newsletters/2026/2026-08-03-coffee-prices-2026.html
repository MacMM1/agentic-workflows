Why your coffee is about to get more expensive
The short version
A run of poor harvests in Brazil and Vietnam, the two largest coffee producers, has tightened global supply just as demand keeps climbing. Futures prices have nearly doubled in a year.
Prices by quarter
Arabica futures have climbed steadily each quarter since early 2025, with the sharpest jump following a frost warning in Brazil's Minas Gerais region.
